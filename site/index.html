Why does Windows warn me during install?

Voxden isn’t code-signed yet. A signing certificate costs more than the app earns right now, so SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC” on first run. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is the same file published on GitHub, and the download page shows both clicks. Signing comes once there are enough paying users to cover it.

Do I need a GPU?

No. On a PC with an NVIDIA GPU the local model answers in about a second. Without one it still works on the CPU, just slower on long dictations. Voxden Cloud needs no GPU at all, which is the usual reason people on laptops pick Pro.

What leaves my PC?

On the Free plan, nothing: the speech model runs on your machine. With Voxden Cloud switched on, the audio of each dictation is sent for transcription and the text comes back; your dictionary terms go along as hints. Signing in sends your email address and, if you use Google, your name and picture. The privacy policy lists everything, item by item.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once a speech model is downloaded. Local dictation, the dictionary and history all work without a connection. Voxden Cloud and sign-in need the internet.

How do I cancel, and what about refunds?

Open Plans & billing in the app and choose Cancel renewal. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for and nothing more is charged. Refunds are covered on the refund policy page.
