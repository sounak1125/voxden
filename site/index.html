How it works

Press. Speak. It's already pasted.

  1. 01

    Press Ctrl + Win

    The flow bar appears wherever you are. Press once to start and once more to stop, or hold the keys to talk.

    Ctrl+

  2. 02

    Speak

    Say it the way you'd say it out loud. Fillers are cleaned up and names come from your dictionary.

  3. 03

    It's pasted where your cursor is

    Into the message, the document or the code comment you were writing. No copy, no paste, no switching windows.

    The standup moves to 3 tomorrow. Notes are in the shared folder.

Runs on your PC

Four speech models. Pick the one that fits your PC.

One small model is ready the moment you install. The others download inside the app when you want more accuracy, and you can switch or delete them in Settings at any time.

Your PC or the cloud

Two engines. You pick where your voice goes.

On your PC

Qwen3-ASR or Whisper runs on your own GPU, or on the CPU if you don't have one. Nothing leaves the machine. Works offline once the model is downloaded.

  • Free: 3,000 words a week
  • Your dictionary and history stay local

Part of Pro. A larger model that holds up on names, accents and noisy rooms, with no model download and no GPU needed. Your dictionary still applies.

Privacy

Your audio stays on your PC unless you turn Voxden Cloud on.

On the Free plan your voice never leaves the machine. With Voxden Cloud on, the audio of that one dictation goes to our transcription service and comes back as text. History, dictionary and settings live in a folder on your PC that you can open and delete.
