        Getting started

        Help

        Dictate in three steps

        Works in any app: email, chat, documents, the browser.

        1. 1 Click where you want the textAny text box will do.
        2. 2 Press Ctrl + Shift + Space and talkPress the same keys again when you are done.
        3. 3 Your words appear where you clickedNeed them again? Ctrl + Alt + V pastes your last dictation.

        You can change both shortcuts in Settings › General › Shortcuts.

        Which model should I download?

        Choose your model when you first open Voxden. Parakeet v3 is recommended for a small, fast start. For Hindi, Hinglish, names and accents, try Qwen3-ASR from the Higher accuracy card in Settings › Speech engines. Everything runs on your PC; nothing leaves it.

        Parakeet

        0.6 GB

        Default, fastest

        Small and quick. Recommended at first setup, with multilingual recognition.

        Qwen3-ASR

        4.7 GB

        Higher accuracy

        The most accurate model, and the best with names, accents, Hindi and mixed languages. One larger download, once.

        Whisper

        3 GB

        Middle ground

        Good accuracy in every language Voxden supports, in a smaller download than Qwen.

        Supported languages: English, Hindi, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, and Dutch. Pick up to three under Settings › General; with more than one, Qwen3-ASR, Whisper and Voxden Cloud work out which one each dictation is in. Hinglish writes Hindi in English letters. Parakeet v3 supports European languages; Hindi and Hinglish need Qwen3-ASR or Whisper.

        Optional: make it faster with your graphics card

        You can skip this. Voxden works on any PC without it. If dictation feels slow, one extra download can speed up your model. Find these in Settings › Speech engines.

        • NVIDIA card + Whisper Download NVIDIA GPU support (553 MB).
        • NVIDIA card + Qwen3-ASR Download the Qwen CUDA pack (3.1 GB).
        • AMD card + Qwen3-ASR Download the Qwen ROCm pack (2.5 GB). Only some AMD cards support it. Voxden tells you if yours does not.
        • AMD or Intel card + Parakeet Nothing to download. Set Processor to AMD or Intel GPU in Settings.
        • Anything else Leave Processor on Auto. Voxden already picks the best option it can.

        Each download speeds up one model only. Downloading a pack for a model you are not using changes nothing.

        Is my voice sent anywhere?

        No. Everything runs on your own PC. The internet is only used to download the model once. After that, dictation works fully offline.
