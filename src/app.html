        Getting started

        Help

        Dictate in three steps

        Works in any app: email, chat, documents, the browser.

        1. 1 Click where you want the textAny text box will do.
        2. 2 Press Ctrl + Shift + Space and talkPress the same keys again when you are done.
        3. 3 Your words appear where you clickedNeed them again? Ctrl + Alt + V pastes your last dictation.

        You can change both shortcuts in Settings › General › Shortcuts.

        Which model should I download?

        Choose your model when you first open Voxden. Parakeet v3 is recommended for a small, fast start. For names and accents, try Qwen3-ASR under Settings › Speech engines. These models run on your PC.

        Parakeet

        670 MB

        Default, fastest

        Small and quick, even without a graphics card. Recommended at first setup.

        Qwen3-ASR

        4.7 GB

        Best with names

        Best at catching names and the words in your dictionary. One larger download, once.

        Whisper

        3.1 GB

        Middle ground

        Uses the words in your dictionary, in a smaller download than Qwen. Much faster with an NVIDIA graphics card.

        On this PC, dictation is in English whichever model you use. Hindi, Hinglish and other languages need Voxden Cloud on a Pro plan: pick up to three under Settings › General, and Voxden Cloud works out which one each dictation is in. Hinglish writes Hindi in English letters.

        Optional: make it faster with your graphics card

        You can skip this. Voxden works on any PC without it. If dictation feels slow, one extra download can speed up your model. Find these in Settings › Speech engines.

        • NVIDIA card + Whisper Choose Download 553 MB under Speed up with your GPU.
        • NVIDIA card + Qwen3-ASR Choose Download under Speed up with your GPU.
        • AMD card + Qwen3-ASR Choose Download under Speed up with your GPU. Only some AMD cards support it.
        • AMD or Intel card + Parakeet Set Processor to AMD or Intel GPU under Settings › Speech engines › Advanced. This needs a separate 2.5 GB download.
        • Anything else Leave Processor (Settings › Speech engines › Advanced) on Auto. Voxden already picks the best option it can.

        Each download speeds up one model only. Downloading a pack for a model you are not using changes nothing.

        Is my voice sent anywhere?

        Only if you turn on Voxden Cloud, which needs a Pro plan. With it off, your audio stays on this PC and dictation works offline once your model is downloaded. With it on, your audio is sent to Voxden Cloud to be transcribed.

        Do I need to download a speech model?

        Cloud dictation needs the Voxden app and an internet connection. The Free plan uses a speech model installed on your PC.

        What is the weekly word limit?

        The Free plan dictates 3,000 words in any seven days, on the speech model you chose for this PC. The week starts with your first dictation and the words come back seven days later. Voxden Pro has no weekly word limit and adds cloud dictation.

        What is the welcome offer?

        Your first month of Voxden Pro comes with 1,200 cloud credits instead of 900. It runs from the day you subscribe until your first renewal, and each account gets it once. From your first renewal on, Pro includes 900 credits a month.

        How much dictation do cloud credits cover?

        One credit is one minute of audio sent to Voxden Cloud, so 1,200 credits cover up to 20 hours and 900 credits cover up to 15 hours. Audio is counted in whole seconds, phrase by phrase, and pauses inside a dictation count too, so the time you spend actually speaking comes in a little under those hours. Credits refresh on your billing date each month, and unused credits do not carry over.

        What happens when I cancel?

        Open Manage subscription and choose Cancel renewal. Your paid access continues through the end of the billing period you already paid for, and nothing more is charged.
