## Voxden 1.0.21

A new Insights page and a flow bar that heals itself.

- 📈 Insights, rebuilt. Milestones are now read year by year, with the date you cleared each one, and the apps you dictate into are ranked as a leaderboard with a direction against the period before. The page opens with a short reveal and stays quiet after that.
- 🤖 The Voxden mascot now lives on Insights too, brightening as your milestones and voice profile progress.
- 🛠️ The flow bar comes back on its own if it ever stops responding, back where you left it. Waking from sleep or unlocking the PC re-arms it as well.
- 🎙️ A microphone that never starts no longer holds the app in "arming": after ten seconds the attempt ends with a clear message and the next press works.
- 🤫 Silent recordings no longer produce stray text. A recording with no speech in it is dropped before it reaches the engine.
- ⏹️ The flow bar's controls say what they do: a stop square while recording, a retry arrow after a result, instead of one tick for both.
- 🔍 A diagnostic log for the flow bar, so a report of "it froze" can be told apart from "the app froze".
