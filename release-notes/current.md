## Voxden 1.0.17

Qwen3-ASR becomes the recommended engine, the Help page is rewritten for newcomers, and dictation starts faster while the app idles cheaper.

- 🎤 Qwen3-ASR is now the recommended engine. It transcribes more accurately than Whisper, especially names and accents, and is the default on new installs. If you already use Voxden, your current engine is unchanged — switch under Settings › General to try it.
- 📖 A shorter Help page. It answers three questions in plain words: how to dictate, which one model to download, and whether a graphics-card download is worth it for your PC.
- ⚡ Dictation starts and pastes faster, and Voxden uses far less CPU while idle.
- 🔢 Spoken numbers are written as digits: "twenty five percent" becomes 25%. Turn it off under Writing style.
- 🎙️ The voice training pipeline: recorder, dataset builder, fine-tuning and evaluation scripts.
- 🔧 Various bug fixes.
