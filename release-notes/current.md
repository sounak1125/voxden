## Voxden 1.0.22

A quieter desktop while you dictate, with cleaner window controls.

- 🔇 Calls, videos, games and other playback now go quiet while your microphone is open, including voices in apps such as Discord. Music players with media controls are paused as before.
- 🎵 Voxden restores only the audio it changed. Anything you had already muted or paused stays that way, including after a cancellation or failed dictation.
- 🪟 Minimize, maximize and close now blend into the Voxden title bar instead of sitting inside a darker rectangle.
